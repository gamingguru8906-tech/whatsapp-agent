Privacy policy

Effective 12 October 2026

This page explains what we collect when you use our readings, why, who helps us handle it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Veshannastro is a Vedic astrology and numerology practice run by Shashank Agrawal. This policy covers the Veshannastro watch reading, the watch for the year, the mobile number reading and My readings. Our main website, veshannastro.co.in, has its own policy.

What we collect

When you ask for a reading, we collect what you type or choose in the form:

  • your name, mobile number and date of birth;
  • for the mobile number reading: what is worrying you, and any numbers you ask us to compare;
  • for the watch reading: your strap, the wrist you wear your watch on, what you would like to improve, and the watch details read from your photo (dial colour and shape, metal, numerals, date window, hands);
  • for the watch for the year: the wrist details read from your photo or chosen by you (wrist size, skin undertone and style);
  • whether you agreed to us storing these details, whether you want updates on WhatsApp, and, if you answer it, whether the reading sounds like you, with the times of these answers.

If you sign in to My readings, we also keep the WhatsApp number that sent us your sign-in code and the time you signed in.

Your photos

A photo of your watch or wrist is sent over an encrypted connection, read once by an automated image reader running on our hosting provider, Cloudflare, and then discarded. We do not store your photo or write it to our logs. Only the details listed above are kept.

How we use your details

  • to show you your reading and keep it in My readings for your mobile number;
  • to reply to you when you message us on WhatsApp, with your reading in front of us;
  • to send you updates and offers on WhatsApp, only if you ticked that box;
  • to see how many people use each step of the site, using counts that do not include your name or number.

We do not sell your details and we do not use them for advertising on other websites.

Where your details are kept and who helps us

Your details are kept in our database (Supabase) and in a Google Sheet used by our team. These service providers process them for us:

  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site, reads photos and processes your IP address to deliver pages and limit abuse;
  • Supabase, which hosts our database;
  • Google, which hosts our Google Sheet, provides services we use to answer WhatsApp messages, and serves the fonts on this site (your browser asks Google for them);
  • Render, which hosts the system that handles our WhatsApp messages;
  • Meta, which runs WhatsApp, when you message us or receive our messages.

We may share details if the law requires it.

What is kept in your browser

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The site keeps a random sign-in key in your browser when you sign in to My readings (it works for 90 days, or until you sign out), and a random number for the current tab so the step counts above are not counted twice. Neither contains your name or number.

How long we keep your details

We keep your reading details so you can see them in My readings and so we can help you when you contact us, until you ask us to delete them. Sign-in codes that are not used stop working after 15 minutes and are then removed.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop sending you WhatsApp updates, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Write to us using the details below. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before you withdrew it.

Children

If you are under 18, please use the site with a parent or guardian, who should fill in the form.

Security

The site uses encrypted connections, and access to the database and Google Sheet is limited to our team and the systems we run. No method of storing data is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise it can never be breached.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
