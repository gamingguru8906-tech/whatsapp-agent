## 2025 as a Numerology “Year 9”

The speaker’s central premise is that 2025 reduces to **9**, which he associates with Mars, purification, sustained effort, and transformation. In this interpretation, the year rewards people who work sincerely and consistently rather than looking for shortcuts; the speaker also describes its energy as highly active. These are the speaker’s numerological claims, not independently established forecasts.

He recommends that everyone make time for themselves before starting the day, arguing that rushing can create anxiety and mistakes. He defines spirituality here in practical terms: taking time for self-reflection and self-improvement, rather than donating only to gain a benefit or to satisfy one’s ego.

Physical activity is another general recommendation: the speaker urges people to keep exercising, even when ill or facing difficulties, and links exertion and sweating with greater rewards in a Mars-associated year. ## Calculating a Personal Year

The method presented is to add the **day of birth**, **month of birth**, and the digits of the year being considered, then reduce the result to a single digit. The speaker says not to include the birth year. For 2025, he uses its digits, **2 + 0 + 2 + 5**, which total 9; this is then added to the birth day and month before reducing.

The examples clarify that the day and month may be added as written or reduced during the calculation, as long as the final total is reduced to one digit:
- **1 August:** 1 + 8 + 2025’s digits (9) = 18, then 1 + 8 = **9**.
- **2 November:** 2 + 11 + 9 = 22, then 2 + 2 = **4**.
- **5 March:** 5 + 3 + 9 = 17, then 1 + 7 = **8**.
- **18 November:** 18 + 11 + 9 = 38, then 3 + 8 = **11**, then 1 + 1 = **2**.

The speaker distinguishes this personal-year reading from a reading based on **mulank** (birth number) or **bhagyank** (destiny number): in this part of the talk, he is interpreting the personal-year result.

## How to calculate the 2025 personal year

The speaker distinguishes a **personal year number** from a birth-based number such as the *mulank* (birth number): to calculate the personal year, add the birth **day**, birth **month**, and the year **2025**, then reduce the result to a single digit. For example, someone born on 10 October adds 10 + 10 + 2025; the total is 2045, whose digits sum to 11 and then 2. The video also demonstrates the method by adding the day and month to 25, treating 2025 as 2 + 0 + 2 + 5 = 9.

Use the same approach for each year rather than carrying forward the previous year’s result: the speaker tells viewers to use 2025, not 2024, when calculating their number.
